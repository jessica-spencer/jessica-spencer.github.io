About Me

I use data, statistics, and technology to understand complex social problems and turn evidence into useful decisions.

My background spans statistical analysis, machine learning, data visualization, software development, and research. I work primarily with Python, R, and SQL and enjoy translating complicated data into clear, actionable insights.

My academic background includes a Master’s in Applied Statistics for Social Science Research and a dual Bachelor’s in Computer Science and American Studies. Together, these fields have shaped how I think about the intersection of data, technology, policy, and society.

I am also interested in social work, equity, and justice. My work increasingly sits at the intersection of quantitative analysis, human experience, and the systems that shape people's lives.

Projects

Gavi Vaccination Impacts: A Propensity Score Study

R Causal Inference Propensity Score Matching

Causal analysis of the Global Alliance for Vaccines and Immunisation using a doubly-robust propensity score design. I analyzed the impact on mortality rates in countries with less than $1000 gross net income.

Volunteering and Pet Ownership?

Python Data Cleaning Missingness Analysis Data Visualization Logistic Regression Prediction

Analysis of Edmonton data on demographics, pet ownership, and volunteering. Higher education had a small positive link to volunteering, while pet ownership did not relate to volunteering. The model had limited explanatory power.

Increasing Contraceptive Choice: Exploring Effective Interventions in Indonesia

R Impact Analysis Multiple Logistic Regression Prediction

An analysis of interventions to increase contraceptive use in Indonesia. The study found that education increased contraceptive use, particularly over the long term, across ages.

Time Series Causal Analysis

Python Time Series Analysis Granger Causality

Granger causality analysis examining the relationship between residential construction costs and the number of households in the United States over time.

Unicorn Companies: SQL Analysis

SQL Data Analysis

Survey Design Survey Administration Research Literature Review Qualitative Analysis
